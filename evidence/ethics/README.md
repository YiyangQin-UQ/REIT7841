# Retained participant information and blank consent form

- [Participant Information Sheet, version 1.0](participant_information_sheet_v1.pdf): four pages.
- [Blank Participant Consent Form, version 1.0](participant_consent_form_blank_v1.pdf): one page; participant name, organisation, email, date and signature fields are blank, and the consent checkbox is unchecked.

These are byte-for-byte copies of the retained project PDFs. They contain the researcher names and University of Queensland contact information printed on the original forms. They contain no completed participant records. The 12 signed forms and identity records remain separately retained and are not included here.

The version 1.0 documents describe the physical Tangram comparison between natural viewing and a shared external camera view. They are retained documents, not a newly revised description of all four study conditions, an updated ethics approval, or permission to release identifiable recordings. Their text has not been rewritten to match later project changes.

The information sheet describes separate storage of consent forms and research data, secure UQ-approved storage, and access by authorised research personnel. Recording access is handled separately from these blank documents.
