# Observational and interview evidence

- [source_records](source_records/) contains the researcher's original process reports, implementation clarifications and later corrections used by the thesis.
- [coded_episodes](coded_episodes/) contains the retained E-code episode records, with their observation, interpretation and evidence boundaries.
- [interviews](interviews/) contains the six group transcript texts used in the analysis. They retain transcription noise and do not establish exact within-pair speaker identity where it was unresolved.

These records retain their historical wording. In particular, the first G06 candle report called the final outline correct; the [later geometry correction](source_records/00_%E5%85%88%E8%AF%BB_G06%E5%AE%8C%E6%88%90%E5%88%A4%E5%AE%9A%E6%9B%B4%E6%AD%A3.txt) supersedes that judgment. Older records also use A/B, preliminary questionnaire coverage and initial protocol assumptions. Read them with the [current corrections and participant mapping](../protocol/README.md). They are provenance, not independent fresh re-coding of all recordings.

Original source files remain untouched. Public copies change absolute workstation paths to repository-relative paths or `retained-locally` source labels. These substitutions are recorded in the publication manifest; scientific observations and values are preserved. Raw video/audio files and signed consent forms are not part of this public package.
