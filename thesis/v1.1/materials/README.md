# Thesis source catalogue

This catalogue preserves the thesis source identifiers D01–D74 without publishing local paths or restricted research records. The thesis PDF links to these entries. The descriptions match Appendix A.

The public thesis and its figures are in this repository. The original videos, audio, signed consent forms, participant identity records, private correspondence and full local archive are not included. An entry in this catalogue does not grant access to a restricted record. Any access arrangement must respect the study consent and be confirmed with the researcher and supervisor.
