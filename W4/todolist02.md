## 5. Turn literature reading into design implications and study metrics  
## 5. 把文献阅读转化为设计启发和研究观察指标

**EN**  
A key point from the meeting was that literature review should not only summarise papers. It should show how the literature informs the design and what variables or behaviours will be examined in the study.

**中文**  
这次会议中的一个重点是：文献阅读不能只停留在总结论文内容，还要说明这些论文如何影响设计，以及实验中具体要观察什么变量或行为。

**Action items / 具体任务**
- Summarise key insights from each relevant paper  
- Write how each insight affects the study design or VR prototype direction  
- Identify possible observation metrics

**Possible metrics / 可考虑指标**
- Coordination  
- Communication  
- Workspace awareness  
- Grounding difficulty  
- Task completion behaviour  
- Clarification frequency  
- Spatial understanding
